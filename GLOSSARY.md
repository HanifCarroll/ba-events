# BA Eventos

BA Eventos is an agenda for independent culture in the City of Buenos Aires, for locals and visitors. It brings together events from their original sources so people can decide what to attend.

## Language

### Discovery

**Event**:
One scheduled instance of a show, exhibition, or other activity; two separately scheduled performances of the same show are two **Events** (Spanish: “evento,” or “plan” in social recommendations).
_Avoid_: Occurrence, slot, show as a name for every kind of event

**Event Listing**:
BA Eventos’ record of one **Event**, distinct from the activity itself and from the **Source Post** that announced it.
_Avoid_: Import, source page

**Catalog**:
The collection of **Event Listings** and **Venues** known to BA Eventos, including past listings and listings outside **Editorial Scope** (Spanish: “catálogo de eventos”).
_Avoid_: Agenda, feed

**Editorial Scope**:
The boundary for what BA Eventos recommends as independent culture, regardless of whether an **Event Listing** already exists in the **Catalog**.
_Avoid_: Scope filter, import eligibility

**Agenda**:
The weekly homepage selection of current and upcoming **Events** within **Editorial Scope** that have an **Event Image**, with views such as “Hoy,” “Este finde,” “Gratis,” and “Música” (Spanish: “agenda”).
_Avoid_: Catalog, home feed, calendar as a name for this view

**Agenda Card**:
One item in the **Agenda**, which may show several separately scheduled **Events** with the same title on the same day at the same **Venue**.
_Avoid_: Event, tile, agenda row as a separate concept on mobile

**Event Image**:
The flyer, poster, or photo selected to represent an **Event** in its **Event Listing**, not every image attached to the **Source Post**.
_Avoid_: Artwork, flyer when the image may be a photo

**Saved Event**:
An **Event** someone has bookmarked in BA Eventos (“Eventos Guardados”), not a booking or a confirmation of attendance.
_Avoid_: Booking, RSVP

**Event Chat**:
A conversation with BA Eventos to find **Events** or ask about a particular **Event Listing** (“Chat” and “Preguntar”).
_Avoid_: Concierge, planner

### Places, times, and admission

**Venue**:
The physical place where an **Event** takes place (“lugar,” or “sala” for a room), not necessarily the **Organizer** or the only space at that address.
_Avoid_: Organizer, address, barrio

**Barrio**:
The official neighborhood of the City of Buenos Aires where a **Venue** is located (Spanish: “barrio”).
_Avoid_: Neighborhood in domain names, district, comuna

**Organizer**:
The person or group credited with putting on an **Event** (“Organiza”), not automatically its **Venue**, ticket seller, or posting account.
_Avoid_: Host when the role is unclear, venue as a substitute for the credit

**Performer**:
A person or group credited with appearing in an **Event** (“Con” before the names on the event page).
_Avoid_: Organizer, artist when the credit need not be for a musician or artist

**Start Time**:
The confirmed local clock time when an **Event** begins, independent of its **Door Time**.
_Avoid_: Show time as a name across all event kinds, door time

**Door Time**:
The confirmed local clock time when people can enter for an **Event**, shown as “Puerta” and distinct from its **Start Time** even when the clocks agree.
_Avoid_: Entry time, show start

**Time TBA**:
The timing state of an **Event** with a known date but no confirmed **Start Time** (“Horario a confirmar”), even if its **Door Time** is known.
_Avoid_: All-day event, midnight start, no time at all

**All-Day Event**:
An **Event** described as lasting all day rather than one whose **Start Time** simply has not been confirmed.
_Avoid_: Time TBA, date-only event as an exact synonym

**Hidden Location**:
An **Event** location the source intentionally keeps private, shown as “La ubicación se comparte después de registrarte” when it is shared after registration.
_Avoid_: Location TBA, unknown venue

**Location TBA**:
An unconfirmed **Event** location (“Lugar a confirmar”), as opposed to a **Hidden Location**.
_Avoid_: Hidden location, secret venue

**Admission**:
The terms for attending an **Event**, including any price, contribution, or condition on entry; an unstated price is unknown, not free.
_Avoid_: Price when the terms include more than an amount

**Free Admission**:
**Admission** without a required payment or a qualifying condition (“Gratis”), distinct from **Pay What You Can**.
_Avoid_: Unknown price, a la gorra, free agenda

**Pay What You Can**:
**Admission** without a fixed price, where attendees can choose how much to contribute (“A la gorra”).
_Avoid_: Free, fixed price

**Admission Link**:
An external link where someone can get tickets, register, or RSVP for an **Event** (“Conseguir entradas,” “Registrarse,” or “Confirmar asistencia”), not simply read about it.
_Avoid_: Ticket link as a name for all admission actions, source link

**Source Link**:
A link back to the original **Source Post** or external event page (“Ver post,” “Ver evento,” or “Ver en…”), distinct from an **Admission Link** and from the BA Eventos detail page.
_Avoid_: Ticket link, event link without saying where it leads

### Catalog intake

**Instagram Intake**:
The process of collecting and reviewing public Instagram announcements to propose new **Event Listings**, attach **Source Evidence**, or make a **Catalog Correction**.
_Avoid_: Instagram publishing, collecting when referring to the whole process

**Source Account**:
An Instagram account identified as a **Venue**, **Organizer**, or curator that supplies announcements for **Instagram Intake**.
_Avoid_: Source post, venue account when the account may belong to a curator or organizer

**Watchlist**:
The set of **Source Accounts** enabled for regular collection, not every account BA Eventos has discovered.
_Avoid_: Venue directory, source directory

**Source Post**:
A public post from another Instagram account that can support one or more **Events** or **Catalog Corrections** (“Ver post” in the **Agenda**).
_Avoid_: Social post, observation

**Post Observation**:
The retained version of a **Source Post** as seen during **Instagram Intake**, which can differ from what the live post shows after an edit.
_Avoid_: Source post, event candidate

**Source Evidence**:
A cited caption excerpt, retained image, or official-page excerpt that supports a specific fact about an **Event**.
_Avoid_: Source link, source post as a substitute for a citation

**Intake Candidate**:
A proposal to create or correct an **Event Listing** for one **Event**, or to attach **Source Evidence** to an existing listing without changing its facts.
_Avoid_: Draft, listing, plan before preview

**Held Candidate**:
An **Intake Candidate** that cannot proceed until an issue such as conflicting dates, an unresolved **Venue**, or an ambiguous match is resolved.
_Avoid_: Pending post, rejected event

**Intake Preview**:
The proposed catalog outcome for an **Intake Candidate**: create a listing, update one, attach evidence, or hold the candidate with a reason.
_Avoid_: Social preview, draft

**Catalog Correction**:
An evidence-backed change to facts about an existing **Event** or **Venue**, rather than a new **Event Listing** or a change confined to social copy.
_Avoid_: New event, social rebuild

### Social publishing

**Social Format**:
A recurring BA Eventos roundup with a defined audience promise and presentation, such as the “Hoy” Story, “Este finde” carousel, “Gratis esta semana” carousel, or “Este finde” Reel.
_Avoid_: Agenda filter, platform, campaign

**Social Draft**:
An unpublished BA Eventos roundup in a **Social Format** for a specific date or date range, which can be revised before publication.
_Avoid_: Intake candidate, source post, preview

**Social Preview**:
The version of a **Social Draft** shown for review before it goes out.
_Avoid_: Intake preview, published post

**Publisher Review**:
A check that the claims in a **Social Draft** agree with **Event Listings** and **Source Evidence** before publication.
_Avoid_: Intake review, approval as a substitute for fact-checking

**Social Post**:
A BA Eventos roundup published to Instagram or Threads, as opposed to an external **Source Post** used as evidence.
_Avoid_: Source post, draft
